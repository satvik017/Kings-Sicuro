Candidate Criteria

Eligibility Requirements To Join KSS

Please ensure you meet the basic physical and documentation standards.

Age & Physical Fitness

Age between 18 and 50 years. Physically active, good eyesight, and no chronic medical illness.

Height & Build

Male Guards: Minimum 5'7" (170 cm). Female Guards: Minimum 5'2" (157 cm). Smart bearing.

Education

Minimum 8th or 10th pass. Ability to read visitor names and vehicle registration plates.

Required Documents

Original Aadhaar Card, Bank Passbook copy, 4 passport photos, and Police Verification Form.
